Stub: Receipt Rewards

Support

Stub helps you take the customer survey printed on a receipt and keep the reward code you earn.

How to try Stub

Stub has no account and no sign in. To try it without a real receipt:

  1. Open the Scan tab.
  2. Tap Try sample receipt.
  3. Walk through the sample survey for an invented business, Sample Burger Co.
  4. At Review before submit, check the page, then continue.
  5. Save the sample code and find it in the Wallet tab.

To use a real receipt, point the camera at the survey link, or choose a photo of the receipt. When you get a reward code, save it in the Wallet tab so it is ready for your next visit. You can turn on optional reminders in Settings.

Privacy, in short

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • Receipt photos and survey answers are not saved.
  • Saved codes stay on your iPhone until you delete them or the app.
