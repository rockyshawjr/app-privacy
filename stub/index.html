Stub: Receipt Rewards

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. Last updated November 11, 2026.

Stub helps you take the customer survey printed on a receipt and keep the reward code you earn. Stub is built to keep your information on your iPhone.

What Stub does not do

  • Stub has no accounts and no sign in.
  • Stub does not run its own servers. We do not receive, sell, or share your data.
  • Stub has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Saved codes. When you save a code, Stub keeps only the business name, the code, an optional expiration date, and the location of that business (used for a nearby reminder). These are stored on your device and you can delete them at any time by swiping on a code in the Wallet tab, or by deleting the app.
  • Settings. Your reminder choices are stored on your device.

What Stub uses and then discards

  • Camera and photos. Stub reads the survey web address printed on a receipt. The receipt photo is not saved.
  • Survey answers. The quick answers you tap (for example your star rating and visit type) are used only to help fill the survey you are looking at. They are not saved. When you finish a survey, the receipt photo, the survey web address, and your answers are discarded.
  • Survey web session. The survey opens in a private web session inside Stub. Cookies and site data from that session are cleared when you leave it.
  • Help filling the survey. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Stub uses Apple's on-device language model to suggest answers from the choices you made. This runs on your iPhone. On other devices Stub uses simple built-in rules. You see the survey page the whole time, you can take over at any time, and Stub stops and asks you to review before anything is submitted.

Location

If you turn on nearby reminders, Stub uses your location to find the saved business near you and to notice when you are close to it, so it can remind you to use your code. Stub does not keep a history of where you have been. You can turn this off in Stub's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone. They are not sent through a server.

Services you connect to through Stub

  • The survey website. When you take a survey, the survey page is loaded from the business's own survey website, and whatever is entered into that survey is sent to that business, the same as if you had opened it in Safari. Their privacy policy applies to that survey.
  • Apple Maps. To set a nearby reminder, Stub asks Apple Maps to find the business by name near your current area. Apple's privacy policy applies to that search.

Children

Stub is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact
