RingBoss: Bell to Bell

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026. Last updated November 14, 2026.

RingBoss is a Mac app by Rocky Shaw for wrestling promoters to book shows. You can keep a roster, bookings, events, venues, and sponsors, and print an event sheet. RingBoss is built to keep your information on your Mac.

What RingBoss does not do

  • RingBoss has no accounts and no sign in.
  • RingBoss does not use license keys.
  • RingBoss does not sync your work between computers.
  • RingBoss does not run its own servers. We do not receive, sell, or share your data.
  • RingBoss has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your promotion's data. Roster, titles, events, match cards, bookings, venues, sponsors, story arcs, branding, and related files (such as roster photos, sponsor logos, and archive images) are saved in an Ark-Book Workspace folder on your Mac. RingBoss keeps this folder name so work from Ark-Book Customer Edition still opens.
  • On the direct download of RingBoss, that folder is Documents/Ark-Book Workspace.
  • If you install RingBoss from the Mac App Store, the app runs in Apple's sandbox. Your data still stays on your Mac. Use Open data folder in the sidebar to see the exact location.
  • App settings are stored in a separate Ark-Book Customer folder on your Mac.
  • You can choose Export backup and Import backup in the sidebar to copy this work or move it to another Mac. The backup file is saved where you choose. RingBoss does not upload it.
  • You can delete your data at any time by deleting the workspace folder, or by deleting the app and its data.

Files you add

If you add photos, logos, or other files, RingBoss copies them into your workspace folder on this Mac. Those files are included when you export a zip backup. RingBoss does not upload them.

Purchases

If you buy RingBoss from the Mac App Store, Apple handles the payment. RingBoss does not collect your payment information.

Children

RingBoss is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: rockyshawjr@yahoo.com.
