12 Gauge Design Studio

Support

12 Gauge Design Studio is a Mac app for making event posters, match cards, social posts, YouTube thumbnails, and print pieces like tickets. Start from a size or a template, add text, shapes, and photos, then export.

Getting started

  1. Open Design Studio and pick a size, such as a square post, a story, or a poster.
  2. Add text, shapes, lines, and photos from the buttons under the Layers list, or search Unsplash for a photo.
  3. Click a layer to select it. Drag on an empty spot to select several layers at once.
  4. Choose File, then Save, to keep your design as a .12gdesign file. Use Export to save an image or PDF.

Selecting layers

  • Click a layer on the canvas to select the top one under the pointer.
  • Option-click to step down through layers stacked under the pointer.
  • Right-click and use Select Layer to pick any layer under the pointer.
  • Lock a layer with the lock button in the Layers list so clicks on the canvas pass through it.
  • Hold Space and drag to move around the canvas.

Common questions

Does Design Studio have an account?
No. There is no sign in. If you get it from the Mac App Store, Apple handles the purchase.

Where are my designs?
Wherever you saved them. Use File, then Open Recent, to find recent work.

Does background removal upload my photo?
No. It runs on your Mac.

Privacy, in short

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • Your designs stay on your Mac.
  • The app only goes online for photo search, font downloads, and the optional upscaling model.
